List of abbreviations

The abbreviations used in the book, with their full English names.

About this list

The list includes the abbreviations that appear in the chapters of the book, arranged alphabetically, with the full English name and, where helpful, a brief note. The full-name column is left blank (—) where the book does not give the name in full and it could not be established with certainty, and the note column is left blank where the full name speaks for itself. Names written in capital letters that are not abbreviations, such as those of authors and publishers, are not included.
