Appendix A

Guide to instruments

A concise reference guide to the instruments presented in this book (54 tools and procedures), grouped by purpose, with a pointer to where each is discussed in the chapters.

Before using this guide

This appendix brings together the instruments presented in the book's instrument profiles and in its tables of instruments and their Arabic versions, arranged in eight tables by purpose. For each instrument it records only what the book itself reports: purpose, age range, examiner qualification level and the status of its Arabic version, followed by the chapter in which it is presented. Age ranges, qualification levels and Arabic versions are all given as they stood when the book was prepared; they change as new editions, new Arabic standardizations and new publisher requirements appear, so the publisher and the instrument's own manual should be consulted before any professional use. This guide is no substitute for the full instrument profile in the relevant chapter.

Purpose: Screening Rating Diagnosis Identification Monitoring. A dash (—) means the book does not give this information for the instrument. An asterisk (*) marks an instrument mentioned in the body of a chapter rather than in a profile or table. The chapter number points to the profile or table in which the instrument first appears in that chapter.
